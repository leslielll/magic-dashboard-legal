Magic Dashboard — Terms of Use

Draft — complete and review before publishing or using with live bank data.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Service

Magic Dashboard is a privately operated tool for reading account transaction information, reconciling incoming payments with customer records, and assisting with invoice preparation.

Bank connections

Access to bank information requires authorization through the relevant bank and connectivity provider. The application is intended to request account information only and does not provide payment initiation functionality. Access may expire or be revoked.

Accuracy and review

Automatic payer matching and invoice suggestions may be incomplete or incorrect. The operator must verify transaction classifications and invoice details before taking any action. Live invoice issuance is not enabled by this description.

Security

Private signing keys, access tokens, and banking credentials must be kept confidential and must not be published in source repositories or browser-delivered code.

Availability

Bank data availability depends on the connected bank, provider, permissions, and network connectivity. No uninterrupted access is guaranteed.

Operator and governing terms

To be completed before production: operator's legal name, contact details, applicable jurisdiction, effective date, and any additional terms required by the provider.

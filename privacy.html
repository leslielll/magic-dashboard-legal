Magic Dashboard — Privacy Notice

Draft — complete and review before publishing or using with live bank data.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Purpose

Magic Dashboard is a private account reconciliation application intended to retrieve transaction information from the account holder's Revolut Personal account, identify incoming payments, and support manual invoice preparation.

Data processed

Depending on the permissions granted and information provided by the bank, data may include account identifiers, transaction dates, amounts, currencies, transaction descriptions, payer names, and related reconciliation information. The application may also store customer matching decisions and a list of senders excluded from invoice preparation.

How data is used

Bank information is used for reconciliation and invoice preparation. The application is designed for read-only bank access; it does not initiate payments. Data is intended to be stored in the application's local SQLite database. Bank access is subject to the account holder's consent and the connected provider's terms.

Sharing and retention

Enable Banking and the relevant bank process information to provide account connectivity. Retention periods, hosting details, access controls, and any additional recipients must be documented by the operator before production use.

Your choices and rights

Bank access consent may be withdrawn using the provider's or bank's available controls. Requests concerning stored data should be addressed to the application operator.

Controller and contact

To be completed before production: operator's legal name, contact email, jurisdiction/address where required, applicable legal basis, retention periods, and rights request process.
